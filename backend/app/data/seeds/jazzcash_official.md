# JazzCash Official Knowledge Base

## Section 1: Mobile Account Registration & Raast Service
Source: https://www.jazzcash.com.pk/account
Document Type: Official Guide & FAQ
Applicable Dates: Effective 2024 - 2026

### What is JazzCash Raast?
JazzCash is Mobilink Microfinance Bank's branchless banking platform. Users can register their mobile number as their Raast ID to send and receive real-time payments across all participating Pakistani banks.
- Registration Process: Open the JazzCash App, navigate to 'Raast', and select 'Register Raast ID'. Your mobile number is mapped instantly to your JazzCash wallet account.
- Free Instant Transfers: Sending money to any Raast ID or Raast IBAN incurs zero transaction fees.
- QR Payments: Raast P2M (Person-to-Merchant) QR code scanning is supported across all retail merchants displaying Raast QR codes.

### JazzCash Account Tiers and Limits
- Level 0 (Unverified / Self-Registered):
  * Daily Sending / Receiving Limit: PKR 25,000
  * Monthly Limit: PKR 50,000
  * Maximum Account Balance: PKR 100,000
- Level 1 (Biometric Verification via Retailer or Mobile App Biometrics):
  * Daily Limit: PKR 50,000
  * Monthly Limit: PKR 200,000
  * Maximum Account Balance: PKR 500,000
- Level 2 / Asaan Mobile Account (NADRA & Tax filer status):
  * Daily Limit: PKR 100,000
  * Monthly Limit: PKR 400,000
  * Maximum Account Balance: PKR 1,000,000

## Section 2: Schedule of Charges (Current)
Source: https://www.jazzcash.com.pk/fees-and-charges/
Document Type: Schedule of Charges
Effective Date: July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2026

- Raast P2P & P2M Transfers: Free (PKR 0).
- JazzCash to JazzCash Wallet Transfer: Free (PKR 0).
- Cash In (Deposit) via JazzCash Agent: Free.
- Cash Out (Withdrawal) via JazzCash Retailer Agent:
  * PKR 200 to PKR 1,000: PKR 20 fee
  * PKR 1,001 to PKR 2,500: PKR 45 fee
  * PKR 2,501 to PKR 4,000: PKR 60 fee
  * Above PKR 4,000: Approximately 1.7% fee
- ATM Cash Withdrawal (PayPak / Mastercard Debit Card): PKR 25.40 at 1Link ATMs nationwide.
- Debit Card Issuance Fee:
  * PayPak Debit Card: PKR 1,200
  * Mastercard Classic Debit Card: PKR 1,500
  * Annual Card Maintenance: PKR 1,200

## Section 3: Customer Support Channels & Dispute Reporting
Source: https://www.jazzcash.com.pk/contact-us/
- Official Helpline for Jazz / Warid Subscribers: 4444 (24/7)
- Official Helpline for Non-Jazz Subscribers: 021-111-124-444
- Official Support Email: complaints@jazzcash.com.pk
- Lost Card Reporting: Dial 4444 or navigate to the 'Cards' tab in the JazzCash App to freeze or permanently deactivate your debit card immediately.
- Important Security Warning: JazzCash never requests your 4-digit MPIN, SMS OTP, or CNIC issuing date over telephone calls or social media.
