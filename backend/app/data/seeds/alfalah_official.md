# Bank Alfalah Official Knowledge Base

## Section 1: Alfa Digital Banking & Account Services
Source: https://www.bankalfalah.com/digital-banking/
Document Type: Product Specification & Digital Banking Guide
Applicable Dates: Current Policy (2024 - 2026)

### Alfa App Overview & Features
Alfa is Bank Alfalah’s digital banking mobile application, catering to both existing account holders and new mobile-only wallet users.
- Digital Onboarding: Open an Alfa Digital Account in minutes using CNIC scanning and biometric selfie verification.
- Raast Instant Transfers: Real-time payments directly linked to Alfa accounts using Raast IDs (phone number) or IBANs. Zero transaction fees apply to all P2P Raast transactions.
- Bill Payments & Top-Ups: Over 500+ billing companies, utility providers, educational institutions, and government challans supported with zero processing fees.

## Section 2: Schedule of Charges for Digital Banking
Source: https://www.bankalfalah.com/digital-banking/schedule-of-charges/
Document Type: Schedule of Charges (SOC)
Effective Date: July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2026

- Raast Transfers (P2P): 100% Free (PKR 0) on Alfa app and Alfalah Internet Banking.
- IBFT Charges (Standard 1Link): Free up to PKR 25,000 aggregate per month. Beyond PKR 25,000, 0.1% of transaction amount or PKR 200 (whichever is lower) plus taxes.
- Alfa Wallet Cash In: Free at Bank Alfalah branches and CDM kiosks.
- ATM Cash Withdrawal: Free at Bank Alfalah ATMs. PKR 25.40 at non-Alfalah 1Link ATMs.
- Debit Card Annual Fee:
  * Alfa Classic PayPak Debit Card: PKR 1,500 + tax
  * Visa / Mastercard Classic: PKR 2,600 + tax
  * Visa Gold / Platinum: PKR 3,800 to PKR 6,000 + tax
- SMS Alert Charges: Digital SMS alerts for digital transactions PKR 150 + tax per month (free on Alfa app push notifications).

Source: https://www.bankalfalah.com/contact-us/
