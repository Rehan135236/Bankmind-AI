# Easypaisa Official Knowledge Base

## Section 1: Raast Instant Transfers & Account Opening
Source: https://easypaisa.com.pk/Send-Money-through-RAAST/
Document Type: Official Guide & FAQ
Applicable Dates: Effective 2024 - 2026

### What is Raast on Easypaisa?
Raast is Pakistan's instant payment system developed by State Bank of Pakistan. Easypaisa allows all registered mobile wallet users to link their mobile number as a Raast ID.
- Linking Raast ID: Open Easypaisa App -> Tap on 'Raast' -> Link your mobile number to your Easypaisa Mobile Account.
- Sending Money: Enter the receiver's Raast ID (Mobile Number) or Raast IBAN. The funds are transferred in real-time, 24/7, with zero transaction fee.
- Receiving Money: Anyone from any bank in Pakistan can send funds directly using your registered mobile number as your Raast ID.

### Account Tiers and Transaction Limits
- Level 0 (Basic Non-Biometric):
  * Daily Limit: PKR 25,000
  * Monthly Limit: PKR 50,000
  * Yearly Limit: PKR 200,000
- Level 1 (Biometric Verified via App or Retailer):
  * Daily Limit: PKR 50,000
  * Monthly Limit: PKR 200,000
  * Yearly Limit: PKR 1,200,000
- Level 2 / Asaan Digital Account (NADRA & Income Verification):
  * Daily Limit: PKR 100,000
  * Monthly Limit: PKR 400,000
  * Yearly Limit: PKR 2,400,000

## Section 2: Schedule of Charges & Fees (Current)
Source: https://easypaisa.com.pk/fees-and-charges/
Document Type: Schedule of Charges
Effective Date: July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2026

- Raast P2P Transfers: Free of charge (PKR 0).
- Easypaisa to Easypaisa Wallet Transfer: Free of charge (PKR 0).
- Inter-Bank Funds Transfer (IBFT via 1Link): Free up to PKR 25,000 per month (as per SBP regulations). Above PKR 25,000, 0.1% or PKR 200 (whichever is lower) applies.
- Cash Deposit into Easypaisa Account: Free at all registered Easypaisa retailer shops.
- ATM Cash Withdrawal (Easypaisa PayPak / UnionPay Debit Card): PKR 25.40 per transaction at 1Link member ATMs.
- Debit Card Issuance Fee:
  * PayPak Debit Card: PKR 1,200 (Annual maintenance: PKR 1,000)
  * UnionPay Debit Card: PKR 1,000
  * Plastic Card Replacement: PKR 1,000

## Section 3: Customer Support, Lost Card, & Fraud Reporting
Source: https://easypaisa.com.pk/contact-us/
- Official Helpline for Telenor Users: 3737 (24/7 toll-free)
- Official Helpline for Other Networks: 042-111-003737
- Official Email: support@easypaisa.com.pk
- Reporting Lost/Stolen Card: Immediately log into Easypaisa App -> My Account -> Manage Cards -> Block Card, or dial 3737 immediately. Never share your 5-digit PIN or OTP with anyone, including Easypaisa helpline agents.
