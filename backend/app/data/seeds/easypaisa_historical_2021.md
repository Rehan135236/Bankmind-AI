### Historical IBFT Fees (Year 2021)
- IBFT transactions under old 2021 framework incurred a flat fee of PKR 50 for transfers up to PKR 10,000, and PKR 100 for transfers above PKR 10,000.
- Old PayPak Debit Card issuance fee was PKR 600 in 2021.
- Raast P2P was in pilot phase.
