# Meezan Bank Official Knowledge Base

## Section 1: Islamic Banking Principles & Deposit Accounts
Source: https://www.meezanbank.com/personal-banking/
Document Type: Islamic Banking Product Guide
Applicable Dates: Current Policy (2024 - 2026)

### Shariah-Compliant Banking Distinction
Meezan Bank is the largest Islamic Commercial Bank in Pakistan, operating strictly under the supervision of a dedicated Shariah Supervisory Board.
- No Interest (Riba): Meezan Bank does not charge or pay interest.
- Current Accounts: Governed by the Islamic principle of 'Qard' (loan without interest). Your deposited principal is 100% guaranteed, and no interest/profit is paid or charged.
- Savings Accounts: Governed by the Islamic contract of 'Mudarabah' (partnership where depositor provides capital and bank acts as managing partner). Monthly profits are distributed based on declared monthly weightages and gross income from Halal financing assets, not a fixed guaranteed percentage.
- Asaan Account: Simplified account opening requiring only an original CNIC and proof of profession, available in both Current and Savings options.

## Section 2: Digital Banking, Raast, & Schedule of Charges
Source: https://www.meezanbank.com/downloads/
Document Type: Schedule of Charges (SOC)
Effective Date: July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2026

- Raast Instant Transfers: 100% Free via Meezan Mobile App and Meezan Internet Banking. No transaction charges apply for P2P transfers.
- Inter-Bank Funds Transfer (IBFT): Free up to PKR 25,000 per month per account (pursuant to SBP regulations). Above PKR 25,000, 0.1% of transaction amount or PKR 200 (whichever is lower) is charged.
- Cheque Book Charges: PKR 18 per leaf (plus applicable sales tax).
- Debit Card Annual Fee:
  * Visa Classic Debit Card: PKR 2,400 + tax
  * Visa Gold Debit Card: PKR 3,200 + tax
  * Mastercard Titanium / Platinum: PKR 4,500 - 6,000 + tax
  * PayPak Debit Card: PKR 1,500 + tax
- ATM Cash Withdrawal: Free at all 1,000+ Meezan Bank ATMs. PKR 25.40 at other 1Link member bank ATMs.
