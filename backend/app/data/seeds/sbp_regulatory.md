# State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Regulatory Guidelines

## Section 1: Raast National Payment Rails Framework
Source: https://www.sbp.org.pk/dfs/Payments.html
Document Type: Regulatory Circular & Standard
Applicable Dates: Current Mandate (2023 - 2026)

### SBP Directive on Raast Instant Transfers
The State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) has established Raast as the sovereign instant payment highway to promote financial inclusion and digitize payments nationwide.
- Mandatory Zero Charges: All commercial banks, microfinance banks, and Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) are strictly directed NOT to charge any transaction fee to retail consumers for sending or receiving Person-to-Person (P2P) transfers via Raast.
- Universal ID Mapping: All regulated financial institutions must provide customers the capability to link their registered mobile phone numbers to their bank account/wallet as their Raast ID.
- Availability: Raast services must remain operational 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year without scheduled maintenance downtime during business hours.

## Section 2: Consumer Protection & Digital Transaction Charges Circular
Source: https://www.sbp.org.pk/psd/2021/C2.htm
Document Type: SBP PSD Circular No. 02 of 2021
Effective Date: Active Regulation

### Inter-Bank Fund Transfer (IBFT) Fee Ceiling
- Free Monthly Threshold: All banks and EMIs must offer free Inter-Bank Fund Transfers (IBFT) up to a minimum threshold of PKR 25,000 per month, per account.
- Fee Cap Above Threshold: For cumulative monthly transfers exceeding PKR 25,000, institutions may charge a maximum fee capped at 0.1% of the transaction amount or PKR 200, whichever is lower.
- Real-Time Alerts: Regulated institutions must dispatch instant transaction alerts (SMS/Email/In-App Push) for all electronic debits and credits at no mandatory cost to ensure fraud deterrence.

## Section 3: Consumer Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Sunwai Portal
Source: https://www.sbp.org.pk/cpd/index.asp
Document Type: SBP Consumer Protection Framework

- Bank Complaint Turnaround Time: Banks are required by SBP regulations to acknowledge consumer complaints within 48 hours and resolve general banking and ATM dispense complaints within 7 to 14 working days.
- SBP Sunwai Portal: If a bank fails to respond within the mandated turnaround time or issues an unsatisfactory decision, the consumer may escalate the dispute to the SBP Sunwai Portal (sunwai.sbp.org.pk).
- Banking Mohtasib Pakistan: An independent ombudsman for resolving banking disputes between depositors and financial institutions.
- Anti-Fraud Warning: SBP reminds all banking customers that neither the central bank nor any commercial bank will ever call depositors to verify account numbers, passwords, OTPs, or debit card PINs.
