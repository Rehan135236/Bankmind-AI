# Habib Bank Limited (HBL) Official Knowledge Base

## Section 1: HBL Freedom Account & Account Opening
Source: https://www.hbl.com/personal/accounts/freedom-account
Document Type: Product Specification & Terms
Applicable Dates: Current Policy (2024 - 2026)

### HBL Freedom Account Benefits
HBL Freedom Account is designed to provide waiver-based banking services for customers who maintain a minimum monthly average balance of PKR 25,000.
- Free Services on Maintaining Balance:
  * Free unlimited Cheque Books (personalized).
  * Free issuance and renewal of HBL Debit Card (Classic or PayPak).
  * Free Online Branch Banking transactions across all HBL branches in Pakistan.
  * Free Bankers Cheques (Pay Orders).
- Fallback Condition: If monthly average balance falls below PKR 25,000, standard schedule of charges apply.
- Digital Account Opening: Customers can open an HBL Freedom Account or Asaan Account paperless via the HBL Mobile App using biometric verification.

## Section 2: HBL Digital Banking, Raast & Schedule of Charges
Source: https://www.hbl.com/resources/filter_all_attachments_anual_embed
Document Type: Schedule of Charges
Effective Date: July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2026

- Raast Person-to-Person (P2P) Transfers: Completely Free (PKR 0) on HBL Mobile and Internet Banking.
- Daily Raast Limit: Default PKR 250,000 per day; can be temporarily enhanced up to PKR 1,000,000 via biometric authentication in the HBL Mobile app.
- Inter-Bank Funds Transfer (IBFT): Free up to PKR 25,000 per month as mandated by SBP. Above PKR 25,000, standard charge is 0.1% of transaction amount or PKR 200 (whichever is lower).
- ATM Cash Withdrawal Charges: Free at all HBL ATMs nationwide. PKR 25.40 at other 1Link network ATMs.
- Debit Card Annual Fee (Standard accounts):
  * PayPak Debit Card: PKR 1,600 + tax
  * Visa / Mastercard Debit Card: PKR 2,800 + tax
  * Gold / Platinum Debit Card: PKR 4,000 to PKR 6,500 + tax

## Section 3: Lost Card Reporting & Helpline
Source: https://www.hbl.com/contact-us
- Official 24/7 Helpline: 111-111-425 (UAN across Pakistan)
- From Overseas: +92 (21) 111-111-425
- Official Email: customer.support@hbl.com
- Reporting a Lost or Stolen Card: Customers must immediately launch the HBL Mobile App -> Navigate to 'Cards' -> Select 'Card Services' -> Tap 'Block Card' or immediately call 111-111-425 to prevent unauthorized transactions.
- Fraud Alert: HBL employees or representatives will never call to request your ATM PIN, NetBanking password, or SMS OTP.
